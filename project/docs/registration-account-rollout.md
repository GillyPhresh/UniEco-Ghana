# Registration account rollout

The student choices are **Buyer** and **Seller**. Standalone **Business** registration stays separate. The existing `student` intent and student referral URLs now lead to the Student Seller lane. Buyers can browse, message, like, comment, save, follow and order using an available non-Paystack method. They cannot publish campus posts or get a referral link. Starting a seller upgrade does not remove free buyer access or enable Paystack for orders. Only that explicit seller registration can use Paystack until payment is verified.

Student sellers use the existing student fee and verified Paystack ledger. Their student seller fee covers later student-business registration; normal student identity and listing approval still apply. Standalone businesses use the existing vendor application and payment path. Historical vendor payments are matched to the vendor's own saved amount and currency, not a newly configured fee. The database rejects a payment from the wrong lane, and an OAuth choice cannot overwrite a returning user's registered lane.
